CANNABIS IN NEW YORK

Visiting from somewhere else? Here’s how it works here.

Cannabis is legal in New York for adults 21 and over, and visitors are welcome to shop at licensed dispensaries. Here’s what to know before you stop by.

01

02

03

04

05

06

  1. 01

    Bring your ID

    You must be 21 or older with a valid government-issued photo ID. You do not need to be a New York resident.
  2. 02

    Buy from a licensed shop

    New York has a regulated cannabis market. Shop with licensed dispensaries like BUZZY so you know what you’re getting.
  3. 03

    Cash or debit

    Dispensaries can’t take credit cards. We accept cash and debit, and there’s an ATM in the store if you need one.
  4. 04

    Know how much you can buy

    Adults can legally purchase and carry up to 3 ounces of cannabis flower or 24 grams of concentrated cannabis at any one time.
  5. 05

    Know where to enjoy it

    Hotels and private properties set their own smoking and cannabis policies. Check the rules wherever you’re staying before using cannabis.
  6. 06

    Keep it in New York

    Cannabis can’t legally cross state lines, even if it’s legal where you’re headed. Plan to enjoy your purchase while you’re in New York.

FAQS

Visiting a Brooklyn Dispensary: FAQs

Is cannabis legal in New York? Read the guide →
  1. 01

    Can tourists buy cannabis in New York?

    Yes. Adults 21 and older can purchase cannabis from licensed New York dispensaries with a valid government-issued photo ID. You don’t need to be a New York resident.
  2. 02

    Do I need a medical card to shop at BUZZY?

    No. BUZZY is an adult-use cannabis dispensary for customers 21+.
  3. 03

    What ID do I need to buy cannabis in New York?

    Bring a valid government-issued photo ID showing that you are 21 or older.
  4. 04

    Can I bring cannabis home after my trip?

    Cannabis cannot legally be transported across state lines, even when traveling between states where adult-use cannabis is legal.
  5. 05

    Can I use cannabis in my hotel?

    Hotels and property owners set their own policies. Check with your hotel or accommodation before smoking, vaping or otherwise consuming cannabis on the property.
  6. 06

    Where is BUZZY in Brooklyn?

    BUZZY is located at 137 Court Street in Cobble Hill, close to Brooklyn Heights, Downtown Brooklyn, Boerum Hill and Carroll Gardens.
