The Best Father’s Day Gifts in Brooklyn’s Cobble Hill

June 194 min read

N° 27 / THE BUZZY GUIDE

Father’s Day is June 21st. Skip the generic gift card and shop local. Every recommendation below is steps from BUZZY, right here in Cobble Hill and Boerum Hill, Brooklyn.

Whether your dad is a wine snob, a comedy nerd, or just a guy who really loves his morning routine, there’s something on this list for him. And yes, we paired every single one with a cannabis recommendation, because Brooklyn dads deserve the full experience.

Abracadabra Dad

He’s been doing the same card trick since 1987 and he’s not stopping now.

Where to shop: Art of Play at 69 Atlantic Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11201

A Cobble Hill hidden gem for premium playing cards, puzzles, and sleight-of-hand essentials. Perfect for the dad who considers himself a showman. Let him browse and he’ll find something that makes him insufferably delighted.

Cannabis Pairing: A creative sativa from Doobie Lab’s (Blue Dream), something that sharpens focus and makes the practice feel like play. Because no trick lands without the right headspace.

The Magic-Loving Dad: Father’s Day Gift Ideas

He has three books going at once and strong opinions about all of them.

Where to shop: Books Are Magic at 225 Smith St, Brooklyn, NY 11231

One of Brooklyn’s most beloved independent bookshops, just a short walk from Cobble Hill. Don’t pick a book for him. Get him a gift card and let him roam. The bookworm dad does not want you choosing for him.

Cannabis Pairing: A mellow hybrid from Ithaca Organics Strawberry Push Pop, the kind that makes a Sunday afternoon on the couch with a book feel like an event. Pair with a cup of tea and approximately zero interruptions.

The Gym Rat Dad

Up at 5am, protein shake in hand, unsolicited fitness advice at dinner.

Where to shop: Heatwise at 163 Atlantic Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11201

Infrared sauna and heat-based recovery, right on Atlantic Ave in Boerum Hill. A Heatwise session is a gift that says “I see your dedication. Now rest.” Already a BUZZY member perk partner, so existing members get even more out of this one.

Cannabis Pairing: Before the workout, Grön Blackberry Lemonade Pearls (3:1 THC) bring a focused, uplifting boost that pairs perfectly with a gym session. Afterward, help Dad shift into recovery mode with Eaton Botanicals’ Coconut Cool Down and Papa & Barkley’s 3:1 CBD Releaf Balm. From warm-up to cooldown, he’s covered.

The Drink in Hand Dad

He swirls his glass before every sip. Every. Single. Sip.

Where to shop: Brooklyn Wine Exchange at 138 Court St, Brooklyn, NY 11201

A Cobble Hill institution. The staff here actually know their stuff, and this isn’t grab-and-go. Send Dad in for a proper conversation and a bottle (or three) chosen just for him.

Cannabis Pairing: Give Dad something new to swirl. HiiStix Beverage Enhancers turn any beverage into a cannabis-infused sip in seconds.

The Well Groomed Dad

His apartment looks like a mood board. His outfits are intentional. He has opinions about chairs.

Where to shop: Goose Barnacle at 91 Atlantic Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11201

A curated retail shop on Atlantic Ave with the kind of finds that feel personal and considered. Perfect for the Brooklyn dad with taste who claims he “doesn’t need anything” and then spots exactly the thing he needed.

Cannabis Pairing: A beautifully packaged, artisan preroll pack from Pure Beauty, because presentation matters to this guy, and he’ll absolutely notice if it looks good on the shelf. Thankfully, it smokes just as well as it looks.

The Dad Joke Dad

His jokes are terrible. He knows. He doesn’t care. This is who he is.

Where to shop: EastVille Comedy Club at 487 Atlantic Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11217

Get him a show, or better yet, take him with you. Brooklyn’s own EastVille Comedy Club on Atlantic Ave is the move for the dad whose love language is laughter. Let the professionals handle the punchlines for one evening.

Cannabis Pairing: A feel-good gummy, Euphoric from OffHours, taken responsibly before showtime. It’s Father’s Day, not amateur hour. (Okay, it’s a little amateur hour. That’s the point.)

For the dad who loves a good laugh, pair it with a comedy special at home and some crowd-pleasing snacks like Space Poppers or NY Infusions Cheese Crackers. Expect elevated moods, big laughs, and zero pressure to take anything too seriously.

The Wake & Bake Dad

He’s got his morning ritual dialed in and he takes it very seriously.

Where to shop: Three of Brooklyn’s best coffee stops are right here in the neighborhood:

  • Stumptown Coffee at 212 Pacific St, the classic and reliable no-fuss pour
  • Octavia Coffee at 198 Court St, a Cobble Hill neighborhood gem
  • Saturn Road at 276 Court St, for the dad who likes his coffee with a little atmosphere

Cannabis Pairing: A morning-friendly, energizing sativa from Breakfast Connections, because some dads have evolved beyond the newspaper. Help him build the full ritual: grind the beans, set the vibe, take his time. It’s Father’s Day. He’s earned it.

Every shop on this list is in the Cobble Hill and Boerum Hill neighborhood, because the best Father’s Day gift is also a reason to get outside and explore Brooklyn. Happy Father’s Day from BUZZY. 🐝
