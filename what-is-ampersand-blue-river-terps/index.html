Understanding Ampersand™: Solventless Dissolvable THC

Ampersand™ is a plant-based, water-soluble powder made from live rosin or ice water hash, dissolved down into a dab you can eat. Blue River founder Tony Verzura built it on a simple premise: keep everything solventless, from extraction through to the final format, so nothing gets stripped or replaced along the way.

Most edibles start with distillate or CO2 oil, flavorless, odorless, and easy to standardize, but they leave the cultivar’s actual character behind. Ampersand™ skips that trade-off. Because it starts as full-spectrum live rosin, the terpenes and cannabinoids from the source plant are still doing the work, not a formulation built to fake it.

Add it to a drink, or let it dissolve straight on your tongue in about two minutes. Either way, it’s designed to disappear cleanly into liquid instead of separating out or clumping, a problem solventless products have struggled with for years.

Why Does Ampersand™ Hit Differently?

Smoking and dabbing send cannabinoids into your bloodstream through your lungs. Traditional edibles take a slower road through digestion, and along the way your liver converts THC into 11-hydroxy-THC, the metabolite behind that heavier, unpredictable edible feeling.

Ampersand™ is built around a different idea: multi-pathway absorption. Instead of relying only on digestion, the formulation pairs the rosin with fatty acids, vitamin D3, vitamin B8, organic fruit juices, and electrolytes from evaporated coconut water. Cannabinoids are fat-soluble, so the fatty acids help usher them in through sublingual and lipid-assisted routes, not just the gut. The result is a layered onset, faster than a typical edible, without trying to replicate the immediacy of smoking.

Blue River puts the bioavailability gain at 3 to 4 times a regular edible, with effects starting in as little as 2 to 5 minutes.

What’s In Ampersand™ (and What Isn’t)?

Ampersand™ is decarboxylated live rosin blended into a plant-based carrier oil, then dried into powder with vitamin B8, organic fruit juices, and coconut-water electrolytes. It’s vegan, gluten-free, non-GMO, and made in an FDA-registered facility with third-party testing for pesticides, heavy metals, and microbes.

Just as notable is what’s left out: no added sugar, no artificial sweeteners or dyes, no fillers, binders, or anti-caking agents. It’s naturally sweet from the fruit juice, not from anything added.

How Should I Dose Ampersand™?

Ampersand™ is not a casual product, it’s built for someone who’s already dabbed their way past what a standard gummy can do. Start low even if you consider yourself experienced; the onset is fast enough that overdoing it can catch people off guard.

Do not smoke this product, it’s built to be eaten or dissolved, and that’s where all of the engineering behind it pays off.

New at BUZZY: Trout & Co. x Blue River Terps

Two of the most exacting names in solventless cannabis just teamed up, and BUZZY has it first. Trout & Co., the Adirondack Mountains extraction house known for growing, washing, and pressing every gram of its own rosin in-house, has partnered with Blue River Terps to bring their live rosin into Ampersand™. This drop lands in two strains: Miami Vice and Z Punch Purple.

Why Trout & Co. Is the Right Partner for This

Ampersand™ only works if the rosin going into it is worth preserving in the first place. Trout & Co. built its reputation on single-source production: the flower is cultivated, washed, pressed, and packaged by the same team, start to finish, rather than passed across outside processors. That in-house control is exactly the kind of terpene-rich, full-spectrum starting material Ampersand™ was designed around.

Miami Vice

Miami Vice is a 50/50 hybrid, most commonly traced back to a Green Crush x Grapefruit cross, built for a tropical, citrus-forward profile that mellows into something earthier on the finish. This is the non-purple half of the drop, worth trying back-to-back with Z Punch Purple to feel how differently two cultivars express themselves through the same delivery system.

Z Punch Purple

Z Punch traces back to Zkittlez crossed with Purple Punch, two parents that reliably throw purple phenotypes and candy-forward terpenes. This batch is naturally purple, colored by anthocyanins released during the washing process rather than any dye, the same plant pigments that show up in blueberries and purple cabbage. Expect a sweeter, more dessert-like terpene profile alongside that color.

FAQ

What is Ampersand™?

Ampersand™ is a solventless, water-soluble THC powder from Blue River Terps, made from live rosin or ice water hash and designed to dissolve on the tongue or in a drink rather than being smoked.

How fast does Ampersand™ work?

Blue River reports effects starting in as little as 2 to 5 minutes, with bioavailability 3 to 4 times higher than a standard edible.

How much Ampersand™ should I take?

Start low, 10 to 20mg, even if you’re an experienced concentrate consumer, and build from there. Effects range from mild at 10mg to heavy at 100mg.

Can I smoke Ampersand™?

No, it’s built to be eaten or dissolved in a drink, not smoked.

Where can I buy Ampersand™ in Brooklyn?

BUZZY carries Ampersand™, including the exclusive Trout & Co. x Blue River Terps drop in Miami Vice and Z Punch Purple. Availability is limited.
